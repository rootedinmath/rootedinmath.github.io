About the program

Free summer math practice for neighborhood kids, taught by three sisters — Reeha, Aashmi, and Aashna — who love math and wanted to share it.

  • Small groups, sorted by grade. Kids work with others at the same level, so nobody feels lost or held back.
  • Short and regular. Two to three sessions a week, 45 minutes to an hour each.
  • Practice over lecture. Mostly working problems together, with help whenever someone gets stuck.
  • Calm and friendly. Because the sessions are led by students, we keep them small and focused.
  • Always free. There has never been a charge to any family, for anything.

What we covered

Because sessions were one-to-one, we taught to each child's math level rather than to their grade, and to what they wanted or needed to work on. Here is what the children covered.

Rising 3rd
  • Multiplication
  • Area & perimeter
  • Clock times
  • Classifying shapes
  • Rounding
  • Fractions
  • and more
Rising 4th
  • Long division
  • Complex multiplication
  • Adding & subtracting fractions, with like and unlike denominators
  • Decimals — adding & subtracting
  • Creating and analyzing bar and line graphs
  • Improper fractions & mixed numbers
  • and more
Rising 5th
  • Divisibility rules
  • Clock times
  • Graphs
  • Long division
  • Area and perimeter for complex shapes
  • Prime factorization
  • and more
